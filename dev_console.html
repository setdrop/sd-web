Onboarding Manager
Pro+ business accounts in their first 30 days. This panel is the operational hub for managing Pro+ relationships during the onboarding period.
When a business activates Pro+ for the first time, an automated welcome message is sent from the Onboarding Manager account within 5 minutes. The Onboarding Manager is available via direct in-app message with a 4-hour response SLA, 7 days a week, for the first 30 days. After 30 days the account moves to Graduated status and onboarding notifications stop, but the message thread stays open.

Inactivity flag rule
Any Pro+ account that has not logged in for 5 or more consecutive days within their first 30 days is flagged with an amber indicator. This prompts a direct check-in message from the Onboarding Manager.
