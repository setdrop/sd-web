The Problem

Booking live music is broken on both sides.

For independent artists, getting work means cold DMs, unanswered emails, and platforms that take a cut of every gig. There is no central place to build a verified track record and no fair system for getting discovered based on merit.

For venues, finding the right artist is time-consuming and unreliable. When a booking does happen, the paperwork is informal, disputes are common, and there is no way to verify who you are actually hiring.

Both sides are losing. The industry is paying for it.

What We Stand For

Built with the scene, for the scene.

Setdrop came from inside the industry — from someone who lived the frustrations independent artists face every day. That background shapes every decision we make.

Artists keep everything they earn
Zero booking fees for artists is not a feature we can remove. It is constitutionally protected. Other platforms take up to 20% of every gig. We take nothing.
Transparency over fine print
Pricing is public. Contracts are generated automatically in plain language. No hidden fees, no surprise charges, no ambiguity about who owns what.
Verified over assumed
Every completed booking is permanently added to an artist's verified career record. You can see exactly who you are hiring and what they have done.
Built to last, not to flip
We are not building this to sell it. Setdrop is designed to be a permanent part of the industry it serves, with policies that protect artists regardless of who is running the company.
